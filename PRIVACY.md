# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Quran is an app for reading and listening to the Qur'an, with prayer times and a few daily tools. It has no
accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains the little that leaves your iPhone.

## What stays on your iPhone
Everything you do in the app is stored only on your device: your bookmarks, dhikr counts, reading position,
listening history, settings, downloaded audio and downloaded translations. None of it is sent to us or to anyone
else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## Location
If you allow location access, the app uses your location for two things:

- **Qibla direction.** Worked out on your iPhone. Your location is not sent anywhere for this.
- **Prayer times.** Your position is rounded to about one kilometre (two decimal places of latitude and
  longitude) and sent to the [AlAdhan prayer times API](https://aladhan.com) to fetch a month of times. The
  rounded position is also kept on your device so the times can be refreshed. Your exact position is never sent
  or stored.

Location is optional. You can read, listen and use every other feature without it, and you can turn it off at any
time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

## Content downloaded by the app
To play recitations and show extra content, the app requests files from these services:

| Service | What is requested |
|---|---|
| quran.com (`verses.quran.com`, `audio.qurancdn.com`, `api.quran.com`, `api.qurancdn.com`) | Recitation audio, word timings, word-by-word meanings, tafsir |
| EveryAyah (`everyayah.com`, `mirrors.quranicaudio.com`) | Recitation audio for one reciter |
| alquran.cloud (`api.alquran.cloud`) | Translations you choose |
| AlAdhan (`api.aladhan.com`) | Prayer times, as described above |

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content (for example, which surah and ayah). Like any
web server, these services can see your IP address when your device connects to them. Their own privacy policies
apply to how they handle it.

## Notifications
Prayer time notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification service or server is involved.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
